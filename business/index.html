Opportunity Map

Key sectors open to foreign investment

Vision 2030 has opened most of the Saudi economy to 100% foreign ownership. These eight sectors are seeing the highest inbound investment.

💻
Tech & Digital

Cloud, fintech, AI, and cybersecurity are priority sectors with direct government procurement and SDAIA support.

🏥
Healthcare

Private hospitals, medtech, and pharmaceutical manufacturing are incentivised as the Kingdom expands capacity for 35M+ residents.

🎭
Tourism & Entertainment

A $1T tourism goal by 2030 is driving massive investment in hotels, theme parks, concerts, and heritage tourism.

☀️
Renewable Energy

50% clean energy target by 2030. NEOM's green hydrogen projects and massive solar/wind tenders are open to foreign developers.

🏭
Manufacturing

Special Economic Zones offer duty-free inputs and land subsidies to attract advanced manufacturing away from China.

🏦
Financial Services

Riyadh is targeting top-10 global financial centre status. SAMA and CMA actively licence foreign asset managers and fintechs.

🎓
Education

Private international schools, EdTech platforms, and university partnerships are in high demand with a young, fast-growing population.

🏗️
Real Estate

NEOM, Diriyah, Qiddiya, and Red Sea Project represent trillions in planned development — with broad supply chain opportunities for foreign firms.

The Transformation Context

What Vision 2030 means for your business

Launched by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in 2016, Vision 2030 is Saudi Arabia's strategic plan to diversify the economy away from oil dependency. For foreign businesses, it represents the most significant opening of the Saudi market in the country's modern history.

The practical results are already visible: new foreign investment laws, streamlined company registration at MISA, 100% ownership in most sectors, and multi-billion dollar giga-projects actively seeking international partners and suppliers.

🏛️
Regulatory reform

MISA (Ministry of Investment) now handles foreign business licensing end-to-end, with online applications, defined SLAs, and a dedicated investor relations team.

🌐
Regional HQ incentive

The Regional Headquarters (RHQ) programme gives multinationals locating their MENA head office in Riyadh a 30-year 0% corporate tax holiday and fast-tracked licensing.

🤝
Local content requirements

Government procurement now mandates Saudi content percentages in most sectors. Smart foreign businesses build Saudisation into their strategy from day one.

Giga-Projects: Opportunity at Scale

Saudi Arabia's giga-projects represent over $1.25 trillion in planned investment. Each creates vast supply chain and service opportunities for foreign companies:

  • NEOM — the $500B+ linear city and tech hub in northwest Saudi Arabia
  • Red Sea Project — luxury tourism destination across 50 islands
  • Qiddiya — entertainment and sports city near Riyadh
  • Diriyah — cultural and heritage district in Riyadh
  • King Salman Park — world's largest urban park in central Riyadh
  • New Murabba — downtown Riyadh's cube-shaped mega-development
