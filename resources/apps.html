Resources — Apps

Essential Apps for Expats in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is one of the most digitised countries in the world. These are the apps you need — from government services and banking to food delivery and getting around.

Absher — your digital ID hub Tamm, Tawakkalna & more iOS & Android available
Must-Have First

Download These Before Anything Else

These government and identity apps are essential from day one. Without Absher, you cannot process your Iqama, manage visas, or access most official services.

Government & Identity
🏛️
Must Have — Government
Absher (أبشر)

The gateway to all Saudi government services. Manage your Iqama, process family visas, pay traffic fines, update personal info, and authorise Iqama transfers. Mandatory for all residents.

💼
Must Have — Work
Qiwa (قوى)

Ministry of HR platform. Manage employment contracts, apply for freelance permits, request job transfers, and view your labour rights. Every employed expat needs this.

🏥
Must Have — Health
Sehhaty (صحتي)

Book appointments at government health centres, access your health records, view vaccination history, and register for health insurance. Links to your Absher account.

🏢
Riyadh Residents
Tamm (تمم)

Riyadh municipality app. Pay municipality fees, report issues, request permits, and access city services. Essential for Riyadh-based expats dealing with housing or business matters.

🚔
Driving
Muroor (مرور)

Traffic department app. Check and pay Saher speed camera fines, renew vehicle registration (Istimara), and check driving licence status. Fines must be cleared before Iqama renewal.

📦
Post & Addresses
Saudi Post / SPL

Track parcels, manage your National Address (required for banking and government services), and schedule home deliveries. Register your National Address before opening a bank account.

Banking & Payments
💳
Must Have — Payments
STC Pay

Saudi Arabia's leading digital wallet. Send money, pay bills, top up phones, and make contactless payments. Works without a traditional bank account — great for new arrivals.

🏦
Banking
Al Rajhi Bank App

Saudi Arabia's largest Islamic bank. Full-featured mobile banking: transfers, bill payments, SADAD payments, and currency exchange. Most popular bank among expats.

🏦
Banking
SNB AlAhli (البنك الأهلي)

Saudi National Bank — largest bank by assets. Strong corporate banking and expat-friendly features. Good for international wire transfers and investment products.

💸
Remittance
Wise / Western Union

For sending money home. Wise offers mid-market exchange rates. Western Union has 1,000+ agent locations in Saudi Arabia. Al Rajhi Exchange also widely used for remittances.

Transport & Getting Around
🚗
Must Have — Rides
Uber

Works exactly as you'd expect. Widely available in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam. Often faster to find a ride than Careem. Surge pricing applies during peak hours and rain.

🟢
Rides
Careem

Middle East-focused ride-hailing. Strong in Saudi Arabia with good driver coverage outside central areas. Also offers Careem Pay and food delivery in some cities.

🚇
Riyadh Metro
Naqd (نقد)

Official Riyadh Metro & bus app. Plan journeys, buy tickets, and top up your metro card. Essential for Riyadh residents who use the metro or public bus network.

Food, Shopping & Daily Life
🍔
Must Have — Food
HungerStation (هنقرستيشن)

Saudi Arabia's dominant food delivery app. Thousands of restaurants in every city. Fast delivery, live tracking, and frequent promo codes. Most expats order daily.

🛒
Grocery Delivery
Nana (نانا)

Grocery delivery from supermarkets including Danube, Panda, and Tamimi. 30-minute delivery in most areas. Great for large grocery orders without visiting the shop.

🛍️
Shopping
Noon & Amazon.sa

The two dominant e-commerce platforms. Noon is UAE/Saudi focused with fast local delivery. Amazon.sa has the widest selection. Both deliver to compounds and residential areas.

🏠
Home Services
Mrsool (مرسول)

On-demand delivery and errand service. Send a driver to pick up anything from anywhere in the city. Popular for collecting items, pharmacies, and same-day errands.

💊
Healthcare
Nahdi / White Pharmacy

Pharmacy delivery apps. Order prescription and OTC medicines for home delivery. Nahdi and White Pharmacy are the two largest chains with same-day delivery.

🔧
Home Maintenance
Helperji / TaskUs

Book plumbers, electricians, AC technicians, and cleaners on demand. Competitive pricing and vetted professionals. Essential for expats in private villas or apartments.

Pro Tip

Set Up Absher on Day One

Absher requires your Saudi mobile number and Iqama number to activate. Register your SIM card first (same day you arrive), then set up Absher. Everything else flows from there.
