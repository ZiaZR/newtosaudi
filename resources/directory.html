Official Government Portals

Key Saudi Government Services

Residency & Identity

  • Absher (أبشر)
    Saudi Arabia's primary e-government portal. Manage Iqama, exit/re-entry visas, family dependants, traffic fines, and more.
    absher.sa
  • Muqeem (مقيم)
    Iqama status, renewal, and expat residency management portal.
    muqeem.sa
  • Jawazat (General Directorate of Passports)
    Iqama processing, re-entry visas, family visit visas. Offices in all major cities.
    jawazat.gov.sa
  • Nafath (نفاذ)
    Saudi National Single Sign-On system. Required for authenticating government service transactions.
    nafath.sa

Work & Employment

  • Qiwa (قوى)
    Work permit management, job transfer requests, employment contracts, Nitaqat compliance tracking for employers.
    qiwa.sa
  • MHRSD (Ministry of Human Resources)
    Labour dispute filing, worker rights, regulations, Saudisation policies, Wage Protection System.
    hrsd.gov.sa
  • MISA (Ministry of Investment)
    Foreign investment licences, company registration, investor visa services.
    misa.gov.sa
  • MOCI Maroof (Ministry of Commerce)
    Business registration, commercial record verification, consumer protection complaints.
    mc.gov.sa

Healthcare & Insurance

  • Ministry of Health (MOH)
    Public hospital directory, health regulations, COVID protocols, SEHA app for appointments.
    moh.gov.sa
  • CCHI (Council of Cooperative Health Insurance)
    Verify your health insurance policy, check hospital network coverage, file complaints.
    cchi.gov.sa
  • GAMCA Medical Centres
    Gulf Approved Medical Centres Association — pre-visa medical exams required in home countries before work visa issuance.
    gamca.net

Finance & Banking

  • SAMA (Saudi Central Bank)
    Banking regulations, consumer complaints against banks, licensed exchange houses directory.
    sama.gov.sa
  • ZATCA (Zakat, Tax & Customs)
    VAT registration and filing, Zakat payments, customs duties, business tax services.
    zatca.gov.sa
  • CMA (Capital Market Authority)
    Tadawul stock exchange regulation, investment product licensing, investor protection.
    cma.org.sa
Embassies & Consulates

Expat Embassy Contacts in Saudi Arabia

Register with your embassy on arrival — they can assist with emergency travel documents, citizen services, and welfare issues.

English-Speaking Embassies (Riyadh)

  • British Embassy Riyadh
    Riyadh Diplomatic Quarter. Consular services, emergency travel documents, notarial services.
    gov.uk/world/saudi-arabia
  • US Embassy Riyadh
    Riyadh. American citizen services, passport renewal, notarial services, emergency assistance.
    sa.usembassy.gov
  • Canadian Embassy Riyadh
    Diplomatic Quarter, Riyadh. Canadian citizen services and travel documents.
    international.gc.ca
  • Australian Embassy Riyadh
    Diplomatic Quarter, Riyadh. Australian citizen services, passport and consular assistance.
    saudiarabia.embassy.gov.au

Other Consulates (Jeddah)

  • British Consulate-General Jeddah
    Al-Andalus district, Jeddah. Serves western Saudi Arabia including Mecca region expats.
    gov.uk/world/saudi-arabia
  • US Consulate-General Jeddah
    Al-Rehab district, Jeddah. American citizen services for western Saudi Arabia.
    sa.usembassy.gov
  • Find Your Embassy
    Saudi Arabia hosts embassies from most countries. Search for your country's embassy via the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs portal.
    mofa.gov.sa
