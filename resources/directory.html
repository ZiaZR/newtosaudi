Paediatric, Therapy & Insurance

Health Insurers, ABA Therapy & Specialist Children's Services

Saudi Arabia's CCHI-regulated insurance framework mandates employer-provided health coverage from day one — but the gap between what a plan covers and what specialist paediatric therapy actually requires is where families get caught. Register with your insurer, initiate pre-authorisation, and contact therapy providers simultaneously. Waitlists for ABA and developmental therapy run 6–14 weeks from initial contact. Do not wait until your children are enrolled in school.

Bupa Arabia
CCHI Class A · Premium Insurer
Nationwide

One of Saudi Arabia's two top-tier CCHI Class A health insurers — the classification that matters when choosing or negotiating your employer-provided plan. Bupa Arabia is the most commonly included insurer in premium expat employer packages and is accepted across all major private hospital networks: HMG, Saudi German, IMC, Dallah, Dr. Soliman Fakeeh, and Mouwasat. Pre-authorisation is required for specialist referrals, ABA therapy, and developmental assessments — allow 1–3 business days per request. Bupa Arabia's 24/7 helpline handles emergency pre-authorisation in real time. Verify that your children are registered as dependents on your policy within the first 30 days to avoid a gap in coverage.

bupa.com.sa ↗
Tawuniya
CCHI Class A · Premium Insurer
Nationwide

The Co-operative Insurance Company — Saudi Arabia's oldest and largest insurer and the second of the two CCHI Class A providers. Alongside Bupa Arabia, Tawuniya is the most commonly included insurer in expat corporate health packages. Accepted at all major private hospital networks across Riyadh, Jeddah, and the Eastern Province. Also offers motor, property, and travel insurance products — useful for bundling coverage under a single insurer after arrival. Pre-authorisation protocols mirror Bupa Arabia: 1–3 business days for specialist referrals; contact Tawuniya immediately on arrival to activate dependent registrations.

tawuniya.com ↗
AXA Cooperative Insurance
Health & Motor Insurance
Nationwide

Saudi Arabia's AXA arm operating as a CCHI-regulated cooperative insurer. A strong secondary option if your employer's plan is through AXA rather than Bupa or Tawuniya — accepted across most major private hospital networks. Also the most commonly used comprehensive motor insurance provider for expats — annual premiums SAR 2,500–6,000 depending on vehicle value and age. Compare AXA comprehensive vehicle coverage alongside Tawuniya and Walaa before purchasing.

axa.com.sa ↗

walaa.com ↗

Saudi Customs rule: Alcohol, pork products, certain medications, and items deemed contrary to Islamic values are prohibited imports — your mover must provide a full restricted items list before packing. Banking & Finance

Banks, Digital Finance & Money Transfer

You need a Saudi bank account for your salary — most employers are legally required to pay via WPS (Wage Protection System) into a Saudi bank. Most traditional banks require an active Iqama. Digital banks (stc Bank, d360) open in minutes with just your Iqama number and a Saudi SIM. Open a digital account in week one; open a traditional account once your Iqama is stamped.
