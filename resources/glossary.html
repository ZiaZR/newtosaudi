Saudi Arabia Expat Glossary

By NewToSaudi Editorial Team

Plain-English explanations of the Arabic terms, government systems, and bureaucratic jargon every expat in Saudi Arabia needs to understand.

50+ terms defined Government, work & daily life Arabic script included
Quick Navigation

Browse by Letter

ABCDE FGHIJ KLMNQ RSTVW Z
A
  • Absherأبشر
    The Saudi government's digital identity platform. Used to manage Iqama, visas, traffic fines, and most official services. Available as a mobile app. See apps guide →
  • ACWA Power
    A major Saudi energy company and one of the largest employers in the sector. Mentioned here as context for the scale of Vision 2030 infrastructure projects.
  • Al-Ula (AlUla)العُلا
    A UNESCO heritage region in northwest Saudi Arabia, home to Hegra (Madain Salih) — Saudi Arabia's first UNESCO World Heritage Site. Growing expat presence in tourism and heritage sectors. See AlUla guide →
  • Amanahأمانة
    Municipality authority. Each city has an Amanah responsible for urban planning, licensing, and local services. Riyadh's is Amanah Riyadh, accessed via the Tamm app.
  • Asrعصر
    The afternoon prayer (one of five daily prayers). Businesses and shops typically close briefly at each prayer time. Asr is usually mid-afternoon.
B
  • Baladiyaبلدية
    Municipality. The local government body responsible for licensing businesses, food safety inspections, and local permits. Separate from the Amanah in some contexts.
  • Bank Al-Biladبنك البلاد
    One of Saudi Arabia's major Islamic banks. Known for competitive home finance (mortgage) products. Popular among expats with Premium Residency status for property purchases.
C
  • Commercial Registration (CR)السجل التجاري
    A business's official registration certificate issued by the Ministry of Commerce. Required for opening corporate bank accounts, signing contracts, and employing staff. Similar to a company registration number.
  • Compound
    A gated residential community, typically housing Western expats. Compounds usually have recreational facilities (pools, gyms, restaurants) and apply different social rules within their gates. See housing guide →
D
  • DIRIYAHالدرعية
    The historic birthplace of the Saudi state near Riyadh, now being developed into a major cultural and tourism destination. UNESCO World Heritage Site and an increasingly desirable address.
  • Diwaniyaديوانية
    A traditional gathering space in a Saudi home, used for receiving male guests. Also refers to informal social gatherings. An important aspect of Saudi hospitality culture.
E
  • End-of-Service Gratuity (EOSG)مكافأة نهاية الخدمة
    A lump sum paid by employers when an employee leaves after one or more years. Calculated at half a month's salary per year for the first 5 years, then one month per year thereafter. A legal right under Saudi Labour Law. See labour law →
  • Expat
    Short for expatriate. In Saudi Arabia, refers to any foreign national residing in the Kingdom on a residence permit (Iqama). Expats make up approximately 38% of Saudi Arabia's total population.
F
  • Fatoorahفاتورة
    Invoice. "Fatoorah Iliktroniyya" is an e-invoice. ZATCA mandates compliant e-invoicing for VAT-registered businesses in Saudi Arabia since 2021. See freelance guide →
  • Fajrفجر
    The pre-dawn prayer, the first of the five daily prayers. Prayer times shift daily based on sunrise and sunset. The adhan (call to prayer) is broadcast from mosque loudspeakers throughout the day.
G
  • GCC
    Gulf Cooperation Council. The political and economic union of Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman. GCC nationals enjoy special privileges in Saudi Arabia, including simplified visa processes and employment rights.
  • Giga-projects
    Saudi Arabia's large-scale Vision 2030 development projects: NEOM, Diriyah Gate, Red Sea Project, ROSHN, Qiddiya, and others. Major employers for expat professionals in construction, tech, and consulting.
H
  • Hajjحج
    The annual Islamic pilgrimage to Makkah, one of the Five Pillars of Islam. Takes place in Dhul Hijjah (the 12th Islamic month). Non-Muslims are not permitted to enter Makkah or Madinah.
  • Halalحلال
    Permissible under Islamic law. All food sold publicly in Saudi Arabia is halal. Pork and alcohol are prohibited. Halal certification is required for all food businesses.
  • Hijri Calendarالتقويم الهجري
    The Islamic lunar calendar, approximately 11 days shorter than the Gregorian year. Used for religious observances and some official documents. Saudi government documents may show both Hijri and Gregorian dates.
  • HR Directorمدير الموارد البشرية
    Your company's HR department is critical for Iqama processing, visa transfers, and GOSI registration. Building a good relationship with your PRO/HR from day one is strongly advised.
I
  • Iqamaإقامة
    Residence permit. The ID card that allows foreigners to legally reside and work in Saudi Arabia. Must be carried at all times. Required for opening bank accounts, getting a driving licence, enrolling children in school, and accessing government services. See full guide →
  • Iqama Mumayazإقامة مميزة
    Premium Residency — Saudi Arabia's long-term residency permit with near-citizen rights. Available for purchase (SAR 800,000 permanent or SAR 100,000/year). See visa types →
  • Istimaraاستمارة
    Vehicle registration card. Must be kept in the car at all times. Renewed annually via the Muroor app. Expired Istimara results in a fine and the car may be impounded.
J
  • Jawazatالجوازات
    The General Directorate of Passports. Responsible for issuing Iqamas, processing exit/re-entry visas, and managing family visit visas. Now largely digitalised through Absher.
K
  • Kafalaكفالة
    The sponsorship system that ties an expat's legal status to their employer (kafeel). Significantly reformed in 2021 — expats can now change jobs after 12 months and exit the country without employer permission. See visa guide →
  • Kafeelكفيل
    Sponsor. Under the Kafala system, your employer is your kafeel. They are legally responsible for your residency. After the 2021 reforms, their control is significantly reduced.
  • KAFD
    King Abdullah Financial District in Riyadh. A modern business district with office towers, residential units, hotels, and a metro station. One of Riyadh's premier business addresses.
M
  • Madaمدى
    Saudi Arabia's domestic interbank payment network, similar to Visa/Mastercard but specifically for Saudi banks. All Saudi debit cards are Mada-enabled. Works at all ATMs and most POS terminals.
  • Mahramمحرم
    A male guardian (husband, father, brother) required for certain religious and legal acts under traditional Islamic interpretation. Most Mahram restrictions for women have been removed since 2019 in Saudi Arabia — women can now travel, obtain passports, and live independently.
  • MHRSD
    Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development. Oversees employment law, work permits, Nitaqat (Saudisation), and the Qiwa platform. Key ministry for all employment matters.
  • MISA
    Ministry of Investment Saudi Arabia (formerly SAGIA). Issues investment licences for foreign companies wishing to establish a presence in Saudi Arabia. See company setup →
  • Muqeemمقيم
    Resident. The Muqeem portal (muqeem.sa) is the official government platform for tracking and managing expatriate residency data. Used by employers to manage their workforce.
N
  • National Addressالعنوان الوطني
    Saudi Arabia's standardised addressing system. Every residence has a unique national address (short address + full address). Required for bank account opening, deliveries, and government services. Register via Saudi Post app.
  • Nitaqatنطاقات
    Saudi Arabia's Saudisation quota system. Companies are classified (Platinum, Green, Yellow, Red) based on the percentage of Saudi nationals they employ. Affects a company's ability to hire expats and renew Iqamas. See work guide →
  • NOC
    No Objection Certificate. A letter from your current employer allowing you to transfer your Iqama to a new employer. Since 2021 reforms, an NOC is only required in the first 12 months of employment.
Q
  • Qiwaقوى
    The Ministry of HR's online platform. Used by both employers and employees to manage contracts, file labour complaints, apply for freelance permits, and view work permit status. Access via Absher. See apps guide →
R
  • Ramadanرمضان
    The Islamic holy month of fasting. Working hours are reduced (typically 6 hours/day). Eating, drinking, and smoking in public during daylight hours is prohibited, even for non-Muslims. Restaurants and cafes open after Iftar (sunset). A spiritually and culturally significant time.
  • Residency Permit
    See Iqama.
S
  • SADADسداد
    The national bill payment system. Pay utilities, government fees, phone bills, and more through your bank app or ATM. Every payment has a SADAD number. Fast and widely used.
  • Saherساهر
    The automated traffic monitoring system. Cameras detect speeding, red light violations, and other infractions. Fines are automatically linked to your Iqama and must be paid before Iqama renewal. Check via Absher.
  • Saudisation
    See Nitaqat. The government policy of increasing Saudi nationals' participation in the private sector workforce.
  • Shura Councilمجلس الشورى
    Saudi Arabia's consultative assembly. Advises the King on legislation and policy. 150 appointed members, including women since 2013.
  • STC Pay
    Saudi Arabia's leading digital wallet by Saudi Telecom Company. No traditional bank account needed. Used for peer transfers, bill payments, and contactless payments. See apps guide →
T
  • Tashgheelتشغيل
    Work permit. Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources. Your employer obtains this before your work visa can be issued. Required before you can enter on a work visa.
  • Tammتمم
    Riyadh municipality app (Amanah Riyadh). For municipal services, permits, and reporting issues in Riyadh. See apps guide →
V
  • VAT (Value Added Tax)ضريبة القيمة المضافة
    15% VAT applies to most goods and services in Saudi Arabia (raised from 5% in 2020). VAT registration is mandatory above SAR 375,000 annual turnover. Administered by ZATCA. See tax guide →
  • Vision 2030رؤية 2030
    Saudi Arabia's national transformation programme led by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. Aims to diversify the economy away from oil, develop tourism, empower women, and modernise the country. The driving force behind most of the changes expats experience.
W
  • Wastaواسطة
    Connections, influence, or "who you know." A significant cultural concept in Saudi Arabia — personal relationships and referrals carry enormous weight in business dealings, hiring, and getting things done efficiently.
  • Work Permit
    See Tashgheel. The official authorisation for a foreigner to be employed in Saudi Arabia, issued before entry.
Z
  • Zakatزكاة
    Islamic wealth tax at 2.5% of net assets, one of the Five Pillars of Islam. Applies to Muslim business owners in Saudi Arabia on business profits. Non-Muslims are subject to corporate income tax instead. Administered by ZATCA.
  • ZATCA
    Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority. The Saudi tax authority. Administers VAT, Zakat, customs duties, and e-invoicing (Fatoorah) compliance. All VAT-registered businesses interact with ZATCA.
