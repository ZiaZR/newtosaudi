Step One

Get a phone line

Almost everything below needs a Saudi number — your bank, Absher one-time codes, delivery drivers. Sort a SIM in your first 48 hours. Here's the honest take on the main networks:

STC
Recommended
The widest, most reliable coverage across the country — the safe default for daily life.
Jawwy
Budget
App-managed plans at lower cost. Good value if you mostly stay in covered areas.
Virgin Mobile
Cheap & flexible
Competitive pricing and easy plan changes — a solid wallet-friendly pick.
Zain
For visitors
Fine for shorter stays, but expect patchy coverage in some areas.
Government
01

Government & Official Services

The non-negotiables. These keep your residency legal and run almost every official task. Set them up in order.

Absher
Government · Start here
Do this first:create your account online, then verify at a self-service kiosk (common in malls). Everything else depends on it.
  • Renew your Iqama and check residency status
  • Issue exit / re-entry permits before you travel
  • Pay traffic fines and manage your driving licence
Website iOS Android
Nafath
Secure login
Needs Absher first:you activate Nafath using your Absher login. It then becomes your single sign-on everywhere.
  • Approve logins to government and bank portals
  • One-tap biometric / OTP authentication
  • Digitally sign official requests
Portal iOS Android
Tawakkalna
All-in-one
Your everything app:digital ID, documents, services and verifications bundled in one place. You'll open it constantly.
  • Carry a digital Iqama and national ID
  • Store health insurance and official documents
  • Access dozens of e-services in one app
Website iOS Android
GOSI
Social insurance
Check it early:confirm your employer has registered you. This is your safety net for work injury and end-of-service.
  • Verify your social insurance is active
  • Track contributions made on your behalf
  • Access work-injury and benefit claims
Website iOS Android
Najm
Accidents
If you'll drive:report minor accidents through the app instead of waiting roadside for the police.
  • File accident reports for insurance in minutes
  • Skip the wait for traffic police on minor bumps
  • Keep your claim record in one place
Website iOS Android
Sehhaty
Healthcare
Great for families:if you've brought kids, this is how you book appointments and track vaccinations and records.
  • Book appointments at public clinics and hospitals
  • Manage children's vaccination schedules
  • View lab results and medical records
Website iOS Android
App spotlight

Absher — your most important app in Saudi Arabia

Absher is Saudi Arabia's official government services platform. Without it, you cannot renew your Iqama, issue exit permits, or access most official services. Set it up within your first week.

Iqama Services
Check expiry, renew, apply for family Iqama, check status
Travel Permits
Issue exit/re-entry permits before traveling outside KSA
Driving & Fines
Pay traffic fines, manage driving licence, vehicle registration
Family Management
Manage dependents, add family members, track dependent visas
💡
Pro tip — register before your Iqama arrivesYou can create your Absher account using your visa/passport number, without waiting for Iqama. This gets you ahead and means Nafath (digital ID) is ready the moment your Iqama is issued.
How to get started
1
Visit absher.sa on your phone
Register with your Iqama or visa number and Saudi mobile number
2
Verify at a self-service kiosk
Found in Jawazat offices, many malls, and government buildings
3
Install Nafath immediately after
Absher activates Nafath — your digital ID and universal login
4
Set Iqama expiry reminders
In Absher settings — notify yourself 60 days before expiry to avoid fines

Daily Life
02

Communication & Getting Around

How you'll talk to people — including government and employers — and how you'll move around the city.

WhatsApp
Messaging · Essential
Non-optional in Saudi:it's the default for work, friends, and even some official and government communication. Install it day one.
  • Primary channel for employers and colleagues
  • Free calls and messages back home
  • Used by many businesses for support and bookings
Website iOS Android
Uber
Ride-hailing
Reliable everywhere:strong coverage in the big cities with fixed, upfront pricing — no haggling.
  • Book a ride 24/7 in major cities
  • See the price before you confirm
  • Track your driver and pay by card or cash
Website iOS Android
Careem
Rides & delivery
Keep both installed:Careem often wins on price or availability, and also does food and parcels.
  • Compare fares against Uber before booking
  • Order food and send parcels in the same app
  • Good reach into residential areas
Website iOS Android
Money
03

Banking

You'll need a local account for your salary. These are the fastest to open as a newcomer — some entirely from your phone.

SNB
Saudi National Bank
Easy to open:accounts can be set up online with relatively little friction — a solid first account.
  • Open an account online once your Iqama is ready
  • Receive salary and pay bills from the app
  • Wide branch and ATM network nationwide
Website iOS Android
stc Bank
Digital bank
Up and running fast:app onboarding takes only a few minutes — handy while a traditional account is being set up.
  • Open in minutes, fully from the app
  • Everyday payments and transfers
  • Pairs neatly with an STC line
Website iOS Android
D360
Digital bank
Modern alternative:a newer fully-digital bank with a clean, app-first experience for daily money.
  • App-first banking with quick onboarding
  • Simple everyday spending and budgeting
  • Good backup to a main salary account
Website iOS Android

Important

Keep one eye on your Iqama

Your legal stay depends on a valid Iqama and your sponsor's renewal. A lapsed permit can mean fines, travel bans, or worse — and it's ultimately your responsibility, not your employer's. Check Absher and Tawakkalna regularly, and set a reminder 60 days before your expiry date.
