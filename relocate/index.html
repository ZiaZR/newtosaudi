Your relocation journey

Four stages to a successful move

Most relocation mistakes happen from skipping ahead. Follow the timeline and nothing gets left behind.

1
90+ days out
Plan & Prepare
Lay the legal and logistical groundwork before you do anything else.
  • Confirm visa type for your role
  • Apostille all key documents
  • Research housing options
  • Start Absher account setup
2
30 days out
Before You Arrive
Packing, customs rules, temp housing confirmed, and app downloads.
  • Medical fitness test done
  • Vaccinations up to date
  • Temporary housing booked
  • Key Saudi apps downloaded
3
Day 1–7
First Week
Immediate priorities that unlock everything else in Saudi Arabia.
  • Buy STC or Mobily SIM
  • Register with your employer HR
  • Iqama application begins
  • Explore your neighborhood
4
Month 1–3
Getting Settled
Build the foundations of everyday expat life in Saudi Arabia.
  • Iqama collected and valid
  • Bank account opened
  • Permanent housing secured
  • Schools enrolled (if family)
