Saudi Premium Residency · Al-Iqama Al-Mumayaza · 2026 Regulatory Framework

The only residency in the Kingdom that belongs to you.

Saudi Arabia's Premium Residency programme grants long-term or permanent residency without corporate sponsorship, without a Saudi partner, and without permission from anyone. Seven tiers. One decision. This is the definitive framework for making it correctly.

Financial Decision Framework

Annual or Permanent? The 8-Year ROI Inflection.

Both tiers carry identical rights — this is a purely financial decision. The breakeven is Year 8. Anyone with a confirmed Saudi horizon beyond eight years pays more by choosing Annual. The Permanent fee is not a premium; it is a discount that compounds with every passing year.

Critical Advisory Note
Annual holders can upgrade to Permanent at any time — but the SAR 100,000 already paid does not offset the SAR 800,000 conversion fee. There is zero credit mechanism. This is a decision to make at the outset, not after Year 7 when you have already paid SAR 700,000 and the horizon is clear.

Key distinction vs. standard MISA Investor Visa: A standard MISA visa is employment-class and confers no property rights, no family sponsorship autonomy, and no exit freedom. The MISA licence is the qualifying gate; Premium Residency is what you receive on the other side of it. These are not interchangeable products.

The KAM Advisory Thesis

The Society of Autonomy

Every senior executive on a standard Iqama carries an invisible liability in every corporate negotiation. Their visa, their family's residency, their right to own property, their children's school continuity — all of it is contingent on a single corporate relationship. Premium Residency removes it from the table permanently.

"Your right to remain in the Kingdom is not negotiated with HR. When you negotiate your next contract, you negotiate from equal ground."

I
Employment Without a Leash
You do not need HR approval to change jobs. You do not need an exit visa to attend an interview in another city. You do not need a release letter from a company that has decided it no longer needs you. Your Premium Residency card is your work permit. It belongs to you — not to your employer's PRO department.
II
Family Stability That Doesn't Clock Out
Your spouse's residency does not expire with your notice period. Your children will not face a visa crisis in January because your employment contract was restructured in December. You sponsor your family directly — not through a corporate PRO team that processes your exit paperwork simultaneously with your own.
III
Property That You Actually Own
Saudi Arabia's real estate market is in a structural pricing cycle Riyadh has not seen before. Premium Residency holders purchase villas, apartments, and commercial property — freehold, in their own name, without a Saudi intermediary. The compound lease renewed annually is not an asset. A registered villa in Diplomatic Quarter is.
IV
Business Capital Without a Partner
You can incorporate a Saudi LLC with 100% foreign ownership. You can hold a board seat without a sleeping partner taking equity in exchange for their passport. You can operate on the Saudi government procurement register as a domestic entity. None of this requires a sponsor. None of it requires permission.
Freehold Rights & Property Framework

What property ownership actually means after Premium Residency.

Premium Residency holders carry the broadest property rights of any foreign national category in the Kingdom. Understanding the geographic scope, the mortgage framework, the title registration system, and the VAT treatment is critical before structuring any acquisition.

PRC Application Walkthrough

From eligibility to card in hand — 9 stages, 30 to 60 days.

All applications are submitted at pr.gov.sa. Do not use third-party agents or portals claiming direct PRC access — the process is applicant-direct only.

01
Tier Identification
Determine the correct tier based on financial profile, professional credentials, and strategic objectives. Applying under the wrong tier generates delays of weeks and potential rejection. A KAM assessment resolves this in one session.
Day 1
02
Document Preparation
Passport with minimum 24-month remaining validity, financial statements (3–6 months), employment letter or business entity documents, endorsement letters for Tiers 5–7, property documentation for Tier 3 (REGA title deed and valuation report).
Days 1–14
03
PRC Portal Submission
Create an account at pr.gov.sa, complete personal and professional profile, select the target tier, and upload the complete documentation package. Incomplete submissions generate review requests and delay the clock by 7–14 days.
Days 14–21
04
Ministry of Interior Background Check
Interpol cross-reference and MOI security clearance. Any prior criminal record in any jurisdiction, Saudi Iqama overstay history, or prior deportation record triggers automatic rejection at this stage.
Days 21–40
05
Medical Screening
GAMCA-equivalent health screening at a PRC-authorised centre — standard blood panel and chest X-ray. Runs in parallel with the background check. Results are valid for 90 days from the date of the test.
Days 21–35 (parallel)
06
Financial Verification
PRC confirms the applicant's financial capacity to meet the relevant tier fee. Bank statements and supporting financial documentation are reviewed. This stage runs concurrently with background and medical checks.
Days 21–40 (parallel)
07
PRC Decision
Approval is issued or a request for additional documentation is raised. Supplementary document requests are common — respond within the window stated in the PRC portal notification to avoid application lapse and restart.
Days 35–60
08
Fee Payment
SAR 800,000 (Permanent) or SAR 100,000 (Annual) via bank transfer to the PRC designated account on approval. Payment instructions are issued directly by PRC — never transfer to a third-party agent claiming to hold PRC funds.
On approval
09
Card Issuance & Activation
Physical Premium Residency card issued within 14 days of confirmed payment clearance. Link the card to Absher immediately on receipt to activate the Nafath digital identity, exit permit management, and direct family dependent sponsorship modules.
Within 14 days of payment
Common Rejection Triggers — Know Before You Apply
  • Criminal record in any jurisdiction — Interpol cross-check is standard
  • Prior Saudi Iqama overstay or recorded deportation history
  • Outstanding fines or financial liabilities registered within KSA
  • Incomplete or internally inconsistent financial documentation
  • Missing endorsement letter — mandatory for Tiers 5, 6, and 7
  • Property with active mortgage encumbrance — Tier 3 specific
  • Off-plan or incomplete asset submitted for Tier 3 qualification
  • Passport with under 24 months remaining validity at time of submission
  • Medical screening result below PRC-defined health eligibility standards
  • Application submitted via an unauthorised third-party portal or agent
