Before you write

Quick answers

The four questions we get asked most often — answered here so you don't have to send an email to find out.

How fast will I hear back?
Within four business hours, Sunday to Thursday. The Saudi weekend falls on Friday and Saturday, so anything sent then is answered first thing Sunday morning (AST).
Which number should I call?
Whichever costs you least. Each advisor has a Saudi line and a local number in their own region — both reach the same person. WhatsApp works from anywhere and is usually fastest.
Is NewToSaudi sponsored by anyone?
No. The site is independent and unsponsored — no government body, relocation agency or property developer pays for coverage here. That's the whole point of it.
I spotted something out of date in a guide.
Please tell us — choose “report an inaccuracy” in the form above. Saudi rules change quickly, and reader corrections are a large part of how these guides stay accurate.
