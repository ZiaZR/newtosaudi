🏗️ What is NEOM?

NEOM is a special economic zone and planned city on the northwestern tip of Saudi Arabia's Tabuk Province, bordering Jordan and the Red Sea. Announced by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in 2017 as the centrepiece of Vision 2030, it is the largest infrastructure project in human history. NEOM is not a single city — it is a region encompassing multiple distinct projects, each with its own character, purpose, and workforce.

The NEOM Projects — What's Actually Being Built

Linear City
THE LINE
A 170km-long, 200m-wide linear city with no roads or cars at ground level. Designed to house 9 million people in two mirrored skyscrapers. Phase 1 (1.2km section) is under active construction as of 2026.
Ski & Mountain Resort
TROJENA
A year-round mountain ski and adventure resort in the Hisma mountains. Elevation 1,500–2,600m. Set to host the 2029 Asian Winter Games. Outdoor ski slopes, year-round outdoor pool, adventure sports, and luxury residences.
Luxury Island
SINDALAH
A Red Sea luxury island destination with yachting, beach clubs, and ultra-high-end hospitality. Target market: international luxury tourism and mega-yacht owners. Expected to open phases from 2024–2027.
Industrial Port
OXAGON
The world's largest floating industrial complex, located at the northern end of the Gulf of Aqaba. Designed to house advanced manufacturing, renewable energy infrastructure, and logistics operations.

Working in NEOM as an Expat

NEOM employs expats through two main pathways: direct employment by NEOM Co. (the state-owned developer), or through one of the hundreds of international contractors and subcontractors working on site. In 2026, NEOM's workforce spans:

  • Engineering & Construction — structural, civil, MEP, geotechnical, project management
  • Technology & Digital — AI, smart city systems, data infrastructure, cybersecurity
  • Architecture & Design — urban design, interior architecture, landscape architecture
  • Operations & Facilities — supply chain, logistics, procurement
  • Hospitality & Tourism — for SINDALAH and TROJENA pre-opening teams
  • HR, Finance, Legal & Commercial — corporate functions at NEOM headquarters

Where Do NEOM Expats Actually Live?

Most NEOM project workers live in purpose-built worker camps and staff villages on or near the site — not in any existing city. NEOM Co. operates its own accommodation infrastructure. Senior staff may be based in:

  • NEOM Bay — the existing coastal area and temporary HQ hub, near Sharma
  • Tabuk City — the nearest existing city (~200km from the main construction zone), some senior staff commute from here
  • Riyadh or Jeddah — some NEOM corporate functions are managed from these cities with regular site visits
💡 The NEOM reality check

NEOM is one of the most exciting career opportunities on earth — but it is a construction project, not a finished city. Expats on site live in camp conditions (high quality by camp standards, but still camp life). The trade-off: exceptional packages, career-defining CV experience, and being part of something genuinely historic. Go in eyes open, and it can be a formative professional chapter.

Packages & Compensation at NEOM

NEOM is known for competitive compensation to attract talent. Typical package elements include:

  • Tax-free salary (no income tax in Saudi Arabia)
  • Accommodation provided in staff villages on or near site
  • Return flights to home country (typically 1–2x per year)
  • CCHI health insurance (mandatory; NEOM covers this)
  • Generous annual leave allowances
  • Relocation allowance

Salaries at NEOM are typically benchmarked 20–40% above equivalent roles in established Saudi cities, reflecting the remote location premium.

Practical Life at NEOM

  • Internet: Starlink and fibre connectivity available at staff villages
  • Food: Catered in camp facilities; NEOM has invested heavily in food quality
  • Recreation: Gyms, pools, social areas in staff villages; Red Sea diving nearby
  • Nearest airport: Sharma Airport (near NEOM Bay) or Tabuk Regional Airport
  • Healthcare: On-site medical facilities; NEOM hospital under construction; serious cases transferred to Tabuk or Riyadh
  • Shopping: Limited local options — most expats rely on camp canteens and online ordering

✓ Pros of NEOM

  • Exceptional salaries and packages
  • Career-defining, once-in-history project
  • No income tax on earnings
  • Strong international, diverse team culture
  • Accommodation, food, healthcare included
  • Red Sea diving and outdoor activities nearby

✗ Cons of NEOM

  • Remote construction site conditions
  • Not suitable for families (yet)
  • Long distance from major cities
  • Project timelines subject to change
  • Limited outside social life at camp

💼 How to get hired

Apply directly at neom.com/careers or through major contractors including Bechtel, AECOM, Jacobs, WSP, ARUP, and Turner & Townsend. LinkedIn is heavily used for NEOM recruitment. Most roles require 5+ years experience in your field and comfort with site/remote living.
