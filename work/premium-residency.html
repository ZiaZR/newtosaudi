Saudi Premium Residency · Al-Iqama Al-Mumayaza · 2026 Framework

The only residency in the Kingdom that belongs to you — not your employer.

Saudi Arabia's Premium Residency programme grants long-term or permanent residency without corporate sponsorship, without a Saudi partner, and without permission from anyone. Seven tiers. One decision. This is the definitive framework for making it correctly.

Financial Decision Framework

Annual or Permanent? The 8-Year ROI Inflection.

Both tiers carry identical rights. The decision is purely financial. The breakeven is Year 8. Anyone with a confirmed Saudi horizon beyond eight years should take Permanent from day one — the Annual tier pays you nothing at conversion.

Critical Advisory Note
Annual holders can upgrade to Permanent at any time, but the SAR 100,000 already paid does not offset the SAR 800,000 conversion fee. There is no credit mechanism. This is a decision to make at the outset — not after Year 7.

The Society of Autonomy

Every senior executive on a standard Iqama carries an invisible liability in every corporate negotiation. Their visa, their family's status, their right to own property — all of it is contingent on a single corporate relationship. Premium Residency removes it from the table permanently.

I
Employment Without a Leash
You do not need HR approval to change jobs. You do not need an exit visa to attend an interview in another city. You do not need a release letter from a company that has decided it no longer needs you. Your Premium Residency card is your work permit. It belongs to you.
II
Family Stability That Doesn't Clock Out
Your spouse's residency does not expire with your notice period. Your children will not face a visa crisis because your employment contract was restructured. You sponsor your family directly — not through a corporate PRO team processing your exit paperwork.
III
Property That You Actually Own
Saudi Arabia's real estate market is in a structural pricing cycle Riyadh has not seen before. Premium Residency holders can purchase villas, apartments, and commercial property — freehold, in their own name. The compound lease you renew annually is not an asset. A villa in Diplomatic Quarter is.
IV
Business Capital That Doesn't Need a Partner
You can incorporate a Saudi LLC with 100% foreign ownership. You can hold a board seat without a sleeping partner taking equity. You can operate on the Saudi procurement register as a local entity. None of this requires a sponsor. None of it requires permission.
Freehold Rights & Property Framework

What property ownership actually means post-Premium Residency.

Premium Residency holders carry the broadest property rights of any foreign national category in the Kingdom. Understanding the geographic scope, the mortgage framework, and the title registration system is critical before structuring any acquisition.

Permitted
Riyadh — All Districts
Full freehold — residential and commercial. Diplomatic Quarter, north Riyadh compounds, Al Olaya CBD corridor, Diriyah Gate adjacents, NEOM community parcels.
Permitted
Jeddah & Eastern Province
Full freehold — Al Hamra, north Jeddah coastal districts, Al Khobar, Dhahran (outside Aramco compound perimeter). Confirm REGA registration zone classification per parcel.
Restricted
Mecca & Medina Holy Zones
Restricted to Muslims and Saudi nationals regardless of Premium Residency status. No exceptions. Boundary defined by Ministry of Interior designation — verify per specific parcel.
Prohibited
Strategic & Border Zones
Prohibited for all foreign nationals including PR holders — military zones, border security corridors, and designated strategic areas. No acquisition is permissible regardless of PR status.

Application Process — PRC Portal Walkthrough

From eligibility assessment to card in hand — 9 stages, 30–60 days.

All applications are submitted via pr.gov.sa. Do not use third-party portals or agents claiming direct PRC access.

01
Tier Identification
Determine the correct tier based on financial profile, professional credentials, and strategic objectives. Selecting the wrong tier delays the application by weeks.
Day 1
02
Document Preparation
Passport (min. 2-year validity), financial statements, employment letter or business documentation, endorsement letters (Tiers 5–7), property documents (Tier 3).
Days 1–14
03
PRC Portal Submission
Create PRC account at pr.gov.sa. Complete personal profile, select tier, upload complete documentation package. Incomplete submissions trigger review delays.
Days 14–21
04
Ministry of Interior Background Check
Interpol cross-reference and MOI security clearance. Any prior criminal record in any jurisdiction, Saudi overstay history, or deportation triggers automatic rejection.
Days 21–40
05
Medical Screening
GAMCA-equivalent health screening — blood panel and chest X-ray at a PRC-authorised centre. Runs in parallel with background check. Results valid for 90 days.
Days 21–35 (parallel)
06
Financial Verification
PRC confirms applicant's financial capacity to meet the tier fee. Bank statements and financial documentation reviewed. Runs concurrently with background and medical checks.
Days 21–40 (parallel)
07
PRC Decision
Approval issued or additional documentation request raised. Requests for supplementary documents are common — respond within the stated window to avoid application lapse.
Days 35–60
08
Fee Payment
SAR 800,000 (Permanent) or SAR 100,000 (Annual) via bank transfer to PRC designated account on approval. Payment instructions issued directly by PRC — never via a third party.
On approval
09
Card Issuance
Physical Premium Residency card issued within 14 days of confirmed payment. Link to Absher immediately to activate digital identity (Nafath), exit management, and family sponsorship modules.
Within 14 days of payment
Common Rejection Triggers — Know Before You Apply
  • Criminal record in any country (Interpol cross-check is standard)
  • Prior Saudi Iqama overstay or deportation record
  • Outstanding fines or financial liabilities within KSA
  • Incomplete or inconsistent financial documentation
  • Missing endorsement letter (Tiers 5, 6, and 7)
  • Property encumbrance for Tier 3 (mortgage not cleared)
  • Off-plan or incomplete asset for Tier 3
  • Passport with under 24 months remaining validity
  • Medical screening result below PRC health standards
  • Application submitted via unauthorised third-party portals
