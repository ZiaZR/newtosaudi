Saudi Arabia Salary Guide for Expats

Tax-free salaries are the headline — but the total package (housing, flights, schooling) is what really matters. Here are real benchmarks across industries, with guidance on what to negotiate.

Package Components

Understanding a Saudi Arabia Expat Package

Saudi Arabia salaries are structured as a basic salary plus allowances. The allowances often make up 30–60% of total compensation and include housing, transport, and sometimes schooling and flights. Always compare total package value — not just basic salary.

Negotiation Tip

When comparing a Saudi offer to a home country offer, add up all allowances to get your total package value. A SAR 25,000 basic with housing + transport + flights + health insurance can be equivalent to SAR 38,000+ in total value. Always negotiate schooling allowance before accepting — it's much harder to add after you join.

Saudi vs. Your Home Country

Is a Saudi Salary Really Better?

For many professionals — especially from the UK, US, India, and Philippines — a Saudi package can represent a significant financial improvement. The combination of no income tax, employer-subsidised housing and schools, and tax-free return flights creates a genuine wealth-building opportunity unavailable in most countries.

A typical example: A UK-based engineer earning £65,000/year takes home roughly £47,000 after tax. A comparable role in Saudi Arabia at SAR 30,000/month (£6,400/month) with housing and flights allowances would take home the equivalent of £76,000+ per year — with nothing deducted for tax.

The catch: quality-of-life adjustments matter. If you have teenagers close to university or strong family ties at home, the personal cost may not make the financial gain worthwhile. Do the full calculation.

Package Negotiation Checklist

  • ✓Housing: cash allowance or company accommodation?
  • ✓Flights: number per year, class, who covered (family)?
  • ✓Health insurance: what is family coverage level?
  • ✓Schooling allowance: per child, per year cap?
  • ✓Transport: allowance or company car?
  • ✓Relocation allowance: shipping, settling-in payment?
  • ✓End-of-service gratuity: confirm statutory minimum will be paid
