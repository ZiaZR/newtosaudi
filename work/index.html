The Process

From Job Offer to Iqama in Hand

Once you have a job offer, the clock starts. Here's the typical timeline from offer to legal resident.

01
Job Offer & Contract
Receive and sign your employment contract. Ensure your salary, allowances, and benefits are clearly stated. Timeline: Day 0
02
Employer Applies for Work Visa
Your sponsor/employer submits the work visa application to the Ministry of Human Resources. Timeline: Week 1–3
03
Visa Stamped, You Fly In
Work visa is stamped in your passport at the Saudi embassy. You enter the Kingdom on this visa. Timeline: Week 3–5
04
Medical & Biometrics
Within 90 days of arrival, complete the mandatory health check and biometric fingerprinting. Book via Absher. Timeline: Week 1–4 in KSA
05
Iqama Issued
Your employer completes the Iqama application. The card is typically ready in 2–4 weeks after all steps are done. Timeline: Week 6–10
